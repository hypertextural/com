Beautiful, functional stool; thoughtfully designed and crafted. Stools may not often be considered beyond base utility — I think we’re better off reaching for more. In my kitchen, the stool doesn’t just help to reach upper shelves: it’s also a place to rest; to hold some odd thing when chaos spills beyond countertops; an invitation to family, who can sit to prepare vegetables, to give company and speed to otherwise mundane work. An object so helpful, so supportive, and so touched by the people we care for that it naturally gathers sentiment — a treasure much greater than thing alone. But that sentiment can’t take root in mass produced plastic, not when we can see the stain of that sad and ugly bargain we’ve made with the factory. So I say let’s choose craft, choose beauty, and choose people before corporations whenever we can.
