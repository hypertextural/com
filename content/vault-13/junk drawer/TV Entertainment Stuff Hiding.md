## Wire grid rack thing

It looks like one thing people do is to use a wire rack, like a grid, and then zip tie stuff to it. A similar idea is a peg board. https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1xUdWaCHiA3cXNa1wk4Kx5NQg-Uq3BPcC

This could work really well for wires… and I think that would be a big improvement, but not so well for game consoles, the synology bay, or a kinect, and I feel iffy about how well it would work for the wifi router.

## Stick it in a box

…or basket, or vintage speaker, etc.

One of the more novel ideas in this group is to stick stuff on the wall and hide it behind a painting. This is nice because it's a two-birds-one-stone kinda thing, but also because the canvas isn't going to block signals. I wonder if you could make a canvas *box*…

Another idea is to just stack books on it, and hide it amongst other things that are similar shape.
