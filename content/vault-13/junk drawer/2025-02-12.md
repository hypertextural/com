Some Ideas / thoughts to explore & write about

- software teams and production as a model for wider work & society
- How the atypical historical conditions of writing software—high skill, limited supply of specialist—led to much higher than normal worker autonomy and how that autonomy contributed to success and innovation both technical and organizational. 
- How software CEOs have gotten rich merely as detritus floating to the top as the workers created value. Success coming in spite of them, not because of. 
- How bureaucracy when allowed can self assemble in the same way, acts altruistically, does not require management. 
- That higher level organization is required to meet broad concerns but that this organization need not have more political control or individual power than those below. 
	- Perhaps groups organized to address broad goals just work exactly the same as those formed to address local goals. 
- Considerations on digital governance, how to support geographically generalized organization without giving it power over everyone else. 
- The open source world and what success in project governance is had there. 
- How “mainstream” media is not equipped to discuss issues outside its context. I.e., discussion of class dynamics outside the scope of partisan politics.
