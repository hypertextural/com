# DevTools metrics

We don’t know what metrics might be most useful yet. We can look for hints externally but who knows how well they will suit us or what we’ll find and, ultimately, any metric can be wielded with malevolence. If leadership has goals to target and a game to play, we can at least aim to win that game while we figure out a direction. So—short term goal is management game and staying alive—mid term goal is finding a new home and defining how we succeed in that environment. If we can collect many metrics for each goal than we can use them in concert to support the narrative that fits the goal set. Alternately we just measure all we can and pick out things that look meaningful—really we should do that anyway, within reason.
