I think you’re right up next to the answer to your question. Recognizing alienation, recognizing reification — it’s easy to forget that the world is what we make it. It’s not what we do at protests, but how we treat our neighbors and ourselves. The privileged among us especially are vulnerable because when we look at the cages of the lower classes we lose sight of our own. We lose sight of the connection between capital domination and our exhaustion, loneliness, and depression, and instead blame ourselves while feeling grateful that at least we don’t have it like _those_ people. Easier to find the energy, I believe, when we recognize that this society only persists as it does because we haven’t made a new one yet, and we can each start whenever we like. WE make the world. WE subjugate ourselves. WE have let a petty minority put chains on our time our energy and our relations, and WE have the power to stop it through each other.
