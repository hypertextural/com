# Physical Media & Digital Purchases

Links: [[Media]], [[Digital Purchases]]

Was talking to Josh about digital purchases and how companies are yoinking them back after someone has "bought" them. This stemmed from a conversation about [[piracy]].

The below was my response and I wanted to save it, because I think it's interesting and something I may want to expand on later.

> Yeah that's really tricky. Sending physical media around does seem like a big waste. I think with stuff like records it can be a little different… they're large and can be artful, and they're a tangible thing that has a bit of beauty in their own right. But CDs, thumb drives? ehhhh no so much.

> Maybe in another 30 years people will have nostalgia for CDs but they just don't seem the same to me. Too technical/digital, maybe? Maybe the rights holders should provide the infrastructure/storage, and if they fail to keep purchased media accessible it becomes public domain. Or maybe this is a case where having some public infrastructure makes sense, like a central library to reduce waste. Don't know! It's really an interesting problem to think about though.
