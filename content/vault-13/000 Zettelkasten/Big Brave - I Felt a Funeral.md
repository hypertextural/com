# Big Brave - I Felt a Funeral

Links: [[Music]], [[Emily Dickinson]], [[Drone]]

I have been really loving this song. It has such a rich, fantastic texture, and I really enjoy the deep drone in the latter half. I tried to find more like it, but haven't managed to yet. The "lyrics" are an Emily Dickinson poem, and they are / it is excellent. I ought to read her. I hope the album has more like this. The [video is interesting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pY0CDcLURRU&pp=ygUQaSBmZWx0IGEgZnVuZXJhbA%3D%3D). But the texture and the mood is just so good. So crunchy.
