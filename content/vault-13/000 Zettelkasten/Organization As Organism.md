# Organization As Organism

Links: [[Gestalt]], [[Zeitgeist]], [[Institutional Memory]], [[Collective Unconscious]]

I think it is interesting how the individual members of an organization do not wholly understand the organization itself, and how the organization over time can take on it's own personality and memory, and how humans can come together to create a sort of hive-being in our organizations.

I wonder what effect the internet has on this, and how our developments in communications enable increasingly large and complex organizations.
