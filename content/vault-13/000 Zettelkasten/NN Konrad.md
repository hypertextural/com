# NN Konrad

Links: [[Typography]], [[Fonts]], [[Roman]], [[Blackletter]]

I like this font a lot, and might like to come back to it for my website. Apparently some [[Gutenberg presses]] made it to Italy in the 1400's and there were cut a Roman style font which is the basis of Roman style fonts today. This font is derived from that type.

It has great character. Modern and not. Feels slightly haunted.
