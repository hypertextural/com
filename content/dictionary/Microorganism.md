A **microorganism**, or **microbe**, is an [organism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organism "Organism") of [microscopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microscopic_scale "Microscopic scale") size, which may exist in its [single-celled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicellular_organism "Unicellular organism") form or as a [colony of cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_\(biology\)#Microbial_colonies "Colony (biology)").
