# Psych Notes, Feb 24

Links: [[Personal Psychiatry]], [[Atomoxetine]]

I met with my psychiatrist, [[Indira Yoder]]. She recommended I try supplementing with [[fish oil]] and [[magnesium glycinate]]. The former [helps with depression](https://www.health.harvard.edu/blog/omega-3-fatty-acids-for-mood-disorders-2018080314414), the latter can help with anxiety and focus.

She also said I could try [[Atomoxetine]], which is a non-stimulant medication for ADD—I do find that interesting, though I am not sure how Amanda would feel about it, and I'm anxious to bring it up. In any case, I don't think I want to fiddle with different medications quite yet, anyway, as I've just gotten stable on the [[Auvelity]].

She also mentioned trying [[oreganol]] (i.e., oregano essential oil) which has apparently also been [shown to have positives effects for depression symptoms](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5842484/).

I am excited to try out the fish oil & magnesium.
