# Learning Calculus

Links: [[Calculus]]

> I used [Calculus Made Easy](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/33283/33283-pdf.pdf) to get started. Realized I got the number crunching part kind of figured out so that I could solve problems but didn't have the depth of understanding I wanted. I decided to go back and try it all again using a combo of [Calculus by Larson, Hostetler and Edwards](https://www.amazon.com/Calculus-Ron-Larson/dp/061850298X) and [Professor Leonard's Calc 1-3 lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLF797E961509B4EB5). His lectures are amazing and the book layout follows the videos pretty much chapter for chapter although as far as I could see so does Stewart's Calculus.
