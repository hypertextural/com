## Sony 65" TV 4K 120Hz XBR-65X900E

$450?

We got this TV in December of 2017 for $1,600. It's gotten something like ~10 hours of use per week since then. The software has been kept up to date. We have a wall mount & bracket for it, as well as the regular stand (I did lose the screws but they're normal machine bolts).

There aren't any issues with it, it's been a good TV. The processor is a little slow which makes the built-in OS a little annoying if you use it, otherwise no complaints. We're only replacing it because wanted to try one of those TVs that shows art when it's off.

It was a well rated TV at the time! https://www.rtings.com/tv/reviews/sony/x900e

I also have the box for it, so I can pull that out if you think it would make it easier for you to move.
