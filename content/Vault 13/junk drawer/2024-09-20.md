Bash: Embrace the Chaos

How the extreme simplicity of processing streaming text allows in the complexity of the real world. 

How closed systems shut that world out and never gain mass appeal. 

Worse is better. 

>  Write programs to handle text streams, because that is a *universal* interface.

Everyone always neglects the last bullet! Especially the *universal* part. 

Another funny thing is that I feel like emacs is just a recursion. Like it’s a mirror in a mirror. I don’t think that’s bad per se. I think the trouble is that emacs is alone. It will never achieve broad appeal and ultimately its sophistication shuts out some degree of complexity. Maybe that can be good sometimes. One thing I would say is that emacs is more interesting than obsidian. Emacs is a platform, everything inside it can be alive; obsidian is just a nice app with a good mobile interface.
