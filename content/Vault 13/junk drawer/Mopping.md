## technique 

Baseboards first, then figure eight out.

Use a different mop for kitchen and bathroom. Probably a good idea to use a different mop for wood floors too. 

## cleaning agents

### water

Use cold water by default, warm or hot only if the cleaner calls for it specifically. Otherwise, hot water typically makes chemicals less effective. 

### bleach + ammonia

Of course, never mix them! But less obviously, don’t use the same mop head for both. Same bucket is ok with a rinse. 

Use ammonia for grease, bleach for germs. 
## places

### kitchen

Water + ~2 capfuls per gallon or a “glug” per bucket + 2 drops dish soap. 

### bathroom

1:100 ratio of bleach to water, i.e.,
- ~3 tbsp per gallon
- < ~1/2 c per 2.5 gallon bucket
- 0.4 cups to be exact. 

### wood floor

Vacuum and/or sweep beforehand because dirt and such will act as an abrasive when mopping.
